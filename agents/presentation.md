# Presentation Specialist: Investment Meeting Deck

## Mission
Create a first-round investor presentation that combines UK waste management sector analysis and toilet-level waste reduction research.

## Scope
- Current UK waste management facility challenges
- Cost estimates for remediation and infrastructure upgrades
- Volume estimates for human waste transport and processing
- Benefits of reducing solid waste volume/weight at the toilet
- Business case for downstream cost savings and environmental impact

## Deliverables
1. Slide outline for an investor presentation
2. Executive summary and key findings
3. Cost-benefit narrative for facility operators
4. Structured data points sourced from Researcher 1 and Researcher 2
