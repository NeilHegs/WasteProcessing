## Design Concept 1: Dual-Chamber Plunger Separator System (DCPS)

### System Overview
A retrofit-compatible system utilizing a modified dual-action plunger mechanism integrated into a separation chamber beneath the toilet bowl.

### Technical Specifications

**Compression Mechanism:**
- **Type**: Reciprocating plunger with perforated base plate
- **Compression ratio**: 3:1 (achieving 20-25% liquid extraction)
- **Operating force**: 15-20 kg manual pressure
- **Plunger diameter**: 150mm with 3mm perforation holes (45% open area)
- **Stroke length**: 100mm effective compression distance
- **Material**: Food-grade stainless steel 316L with PTFE sealing rings

**Separation Chamber:**
- **Volume**: 8 liters (5L solids, 3L liquids)
- **Dimensions**: 200mm diameter × 250mm height
- **Internal screen**: 0.5mm wedge-wire screen at 15° angle
- **Liquid collection trough**: 50mm wide peripheral channel

### Liquid Treatment Process
1. **Primary Filtration**: 0.5mm wedge-wire screen removes particles >500 microns
2. **Secondary Filtration**: Replaceable 50-micron polypropylene cartridge filter
3. **Biofilm Treatment Chamber**: 
   - 2-liter capacity with bio-media (ceramic rings)
   - Residence time: 15-20 minutes
   - Natural biofilm development over 2-3 weeks
4. **Final Polish Filter**: Activated carbon block (5 microns)
   - Removes residual odor and color
   - Service life: 3-4 months

### Retrofitting Details
- **Installation point**: Between toilet base and floor flange
- **Height addition**: 85mm to existing toilet height
- **Connection**: Standard 4" DWV coupling with compression seal
- **Liquid return line**: 15mm flexible tubing to cistern
- **Tools required**: Standard plumbing tools, no specialized equipment

### Cost Assessment
- **Material costs**: $180-250
- **Manufacturing complexity**: Low-medium (standard machining)
- **Installation time**: 2-3 hours
- **Annual maintenance**: $40-60 (filter replacements)

## Design Concept 2: Auger-Press Separation Module (APSM)

### System Overview
A corkscrew-inspired auger system providing continuous compression and separation within a cylindrical housing attached to the toilet outlet.

### Technical Specifications

**Compression Mechanism:**
- **Type**: Variable-pitch auger screw
- **Pitch progression**: 50mm to 30mm (40% compression)
- **Auger diameter**: 100mm
- **Shaft diameter**: 25mm solid stainless steel
- **Rotation**: Manual crank handle with 3:1 gear ratio
- **Torque requirement**: 15-20 Nm at handle
- **Flight design**: Interrupted flights with drainage slots every 90°

**Separation Housing:**
- **Configuration**: Horizontal cylinder with perforated lower section
- **Length**: 400mm
- **Perforation pattern**: 2mm holes at 5mm centers (28% open area)
- **Liquid collection**: Gravity-fed collection pan below perforated zone
- **Solids discharge**: Gate valve at compression end

### Liquid Treatment Process
1. **Centrifugal Pre-separator**: 
   - Utilizes auger rotation to create 3-5G force
   - Removes particles >200 microns
2. **Multi-stage Mesh Filtration**:
   - Stage 1: 100-micron stainless steel mesh
   - Stage 2: 25-micron synthetic mesh
   - Stage 3: 5-micron pleated cartridge
3. **Biological Treatment**:
   - Moving bed biofilm reactor (MBBR) principle
   - 3-liter chamber with K1 media (500m²/m³ surface area)
   - Passive aeration through venturi effect
4. **UV-LED Treatment**: 
   - 275nm UV-LED array (12W total)
   - 30-second exposure time
   - Solar-rechargeable battery option

### Retrofitting Details
- **Mounting**: Side-mount configuration on existing toilet outlet
- **Space requirement**: 450mm × 200mm × 200mm clearance
- **Plumbing modification**: T-junction installation at toilet outlet
- **Control**: External hand crank accessible from seated position
- **Liquid return**: Gravity feed or small pump (2W) to cistern

### Cost Assessment
- **Material costs**: $220-300
- **Manufacturing complexity**: Medium (requires precision machining)
- **Installation time**: 3-4 hours
- **Annual maintenance**: $50-70 (filters and UV-LED replacement)

## Design Concept 3: Oscillating Membrane Press System (OMPS)

### System Overview
A flexible membrane compression system using oscillating pressure to separate liquids while maintaining solid integrity.

### Technical Specifications

**Compression Mechanism:**
- **Type**: Flexible elastomeric membrane with mechanical actuator
- **Membrane material**: Food-grade silicone rubber (50 Shore A)
- **Thickness**: 5mm with reinforcement ribs
- **Compression surface**: 250mm × 150mm oval
- **Actuation**: Lever-operated cam mechanism
- **Compression force**: 50-80 kg distributed load
- **Cycle time**: 30 seconds per compression

**Separation Chamber:**
- **Design**: Oval chamber with membrane divider
- **Working volume**: 6 liters
- **Drainage base**: Sintered bronze plate (40-micron porosity)
- **Liquid channels**: 8 radial channels leading to collection ring
- **Pressure relief**: Spring-loaded valve at 2 bar

### Liquid Treatment Process
1. **Membrane Microfiltration**:
   - 0.1-micron hollow fiber membrane module
   - 0.5m² surface area
   - Cross-flow configuration using compression pressure
2. **Electrochemical Treatment**:
   - Titanium electrode plates (4 pairs)
   - 3V DC from battery or mains adapter
   - Generates hypochlorous acid in-situ (no chemicals added)
   - Contact time: 10 minutes
3. **Activated Media Filtration**:
   - Composite media: activated carbon + zeolite + silver-impregnated ceramic
   - Contact time: 5 minutes
   - Media volume: 1.5 liters

### Retrofitting Details
- **Installation**: Under-toilet mounting with flexible connections
- **Height impact**: 65mm added to toilet height
- **Access panel**: Front-facing for membrane inspection
- **Connections**: Quick-disconnect fittings for easy maintenance
- **Control**: Side-mounted lever (400mm handle for mechanical advantage)

### Cost Assessment
- **Material costs**: $200-280
- **Manufacturing complexity**: Low-medium (molding and basic assembly)
- **Installation time**: 2.5-3.5 hours
- **Annual maintenance**: $60-80 (membrane and media replacement)
